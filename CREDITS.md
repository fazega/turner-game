# Sources and licenses

Scanned models and materials are from [Poly Haven](https://polyhaven.com), published under [CC0](https://docs.polyhaven.com/en/faq). Individual source links are recorded in assets/sources.json.

Models: ship_pinnace, wooden_barrels_01, wooden_crate_01, marble_bust_01, tree_small_02. Materials: seaworn_stone_tiles, large_sandstone_blocks_01, wooden_rough_planks, bark_brown_02, rough_linen, and the twig atlas from pine_tree_01.

Local adaptations include texture resizing/compression, neutral recoloring of linen, cropped sandstone trim, isolated pine needle cards, tree mesh simplification into two distance levels, and scene-specific scale and material adjustments.

Three.js r170 and its GLTFLoader, BufferGeometryUtils, and Water example use the MIT license; see THREE-LICENSE.txt. Water rendering is adapted from [Three.js Water](https://threejs.org/docs/pages/Water.html), with wave techniques informed by [GPU Gems: Effective Water Simulation](https://developer.nvidia.com/gpugems/gpugems/part-i-natural-effects/chapter-1-effective-water-simulation-physical-models).

Tree geometry was processed offline with the MIT-licensed meshoptimizer simplifier.

Character models: [Universal Base Characters](https://quaternius.com/packs/universalbasecharacters.html) and [Modular Character Outfits — Fantasy](https://quaternius.com/packs/modularcharacteroutfitsfantasy.html), by Quaternius, CC0 1.0. The free Standard packs supply the heads, hairstyles, peasant clothing and ranger clothing. Adaptations include removing hidden body surfaces, fitting hair, relaxed rig poses, idle motion, texture resizing and sharing, and merging clothing pieces. Original license files are in assets/characters. Three.js SkeletonUtils is used under the bundled MIT license.
